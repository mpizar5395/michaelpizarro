Booster Pump Station

The basis for this project was to design a new Interconnect Pump Station that could withstand higher water pressures with the intention of expanding the main Water/Waste Water Treatment Plant capabilities. This was my first project managing BIM Coordination among different disciplines, and Modeling among different designers. By the end of it we got the project out on time, under budget, and with the right people!
